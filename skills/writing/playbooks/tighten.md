# Tighten

**You own the shorter text that says the same thing.**

1. Delete before rewording: sentences that do not change what the reader does, restatements, hedges, and justification that manages a problem instead of fixing it.
2. Merge what is stated twice into one place.
3. Reword only what is left, keeping the author's voice and every fact.

**Reply:** what was cut and why, the length before and after, and any fact you were unsure was safe to remove.
